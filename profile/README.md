# Altyzo

Self-hosted, autonomous AI agent infrastructure for SEO, AEO, and GEO agencies.

Altyzo automates the full SEO workflow — technical audits, research, content generation, optimization, publishing, and monitoring — with AI agents you control from a single dashboard.

## Why Altyzo

- **Self-hosted:** your data, your clients' data, and your credentials never leave your infrastructure.
- **Autonomous:** agents work continuously across research, content, and technical SEO without manual intervention.
- **Agency-first:** built for agencies managing multiple clients, with per-client isolation and oversight controls.
- **Full workflow:** from audit to published content to rank tracking, in one platform.

## What you can do

- **Run technical SEO audits** across all client sites on a schedule.
- **Generate and optimize content** with AI, reviewed and approved before publishing.
- **Publish to any CMS** through pluggable adapters.
- **Track rankings and get alerted** when positions change or issues surface.
- **Manage multiple clients** with isolated workspaces and team-based access.
